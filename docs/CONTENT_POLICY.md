# Content policy

## Public content classes

- **Stable mechanism**: a film-language, story, directing, or production principle with an explained causal use.
- **Source-backed interpretation**: an explanation derived from identified evidence.
- **Practical method**: a repeatable workflow with bounded inputs, outputs, and checks.
- **Candidate method**: promising but not sufficiently validated; its status and limits must be explicit.
- **Time-sensitive fact**: product, platform, model, price, feature, specification, or policy information that requires current verification.

## Public boundary

Do not publish personal identity, personal projects, private retrospectives, company or client work, unpublished assets, credentials, raw private chats, full copied third-party works, unlicensed images, local application state, or machine-specific configuration.

The maintainer's deliberately published contact address `haldissita@gmail.com` is the sole contact-information exception.

## Structure

- Theory belongs under `knowledge/A-理论层/`.
- Practice belongs under `knowledge/B-实战层/`.
- Public navigation, policy, and errata belong under `knowledge/00-总纲/`.
- No other knowledge layer is accepted into the public corpus.

## Sources and translations

- Link to primary and authoritative sources when a claim may change over time.
- Mark dead or unreadable links instead of reconstructing their content from memory.
- Keep the Simplified Chinese note as canonical unless its status explicitly changes.
- Store translations separately and link them from the source; do not silently overwrite it.
- A translation may improve clarity but must not add factual claims without updating the source note and evidence.
