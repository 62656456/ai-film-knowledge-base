# 角色与七字段定义
> 用途（标准）：七字段镜头结构标准定义（核心格式依据），所有英文流提示词均按此七字段组织。本卡是提示词工程组的地基卡。

## 七字段总览

每个镜头提示词由以下七个字段按顺序组成，字段之间用逗号分隔。七字段覆盖了AI图片/视频生成所需的全部关键信息维度。

```
主体(Subject) → 动作(Action) → 环境(Environment) → 光线(Lighting) → 构图(Composition) → 风格(Style) → 画质/设备(Camera/Quality)
```

---

## 字段1：主体 Subject

定义画面中出现的人物/物体/角色。

填写规则：
- 必须具体：不能只写 `a man`，要写 `a tall man in his 30s wearing a black trench coat`
- 包含外貌特征：年龄、体型、发色、肤色、服装
- 可选包含道具/配件：`holding a katana`、`wearing round glasses`

示例：
```
a young woman in her early 20s with short platinum blonde hair, wearing a crimson silk dress and pearl earrings
a massive iron golem covered in moss and rust, with glowing blue eyes
a golden retriever puppy sitting, wearing a tiny blue bandana
```

---

## 字段2：动作 Action

定义主体正在做什么或姿态状态。

填写规则：
- 用现在分词或进行时态描述动态
- 静态场景描述姿态/表情：`standing still, looking into the distance`
- 动态场景描述具体动作：`running through rain, turning head sharply`
- 可包含表情和视线方向

示例：
```
standing on the rooftop edge, looking down at the city below
running through heavy rain, coat billowing behind
sitting cross-legged on the floor, reading an old book, eyes focused
raising both arms to the sky, mouth open in a battle cry
```

---

## 字段3：环境 Environment

定义主体所处的物理空间和背景。

填写规则：
- 由大到小描述：先写宏观场景，再补充细节
- 包含时间/天气/季节信息
- 包含背景中的关键元素

示例：
```
abandoned warehouse at night, rain pouring through broken skylights, puddles on concrete floor
cozy wooden cabin interior, fireplace crackling, snow visible through frosty windows, warm rugs on floor
vast desert at golden hour, endless sand dunes, heat haze on horizon, scattered dead trees
```

---

## 字段4：光线 Lighting

定义光线的方向、质感、色温、强度。

填写规则：
- 先写光源方向（查 B2-06 光源方向对照表）
- 再写光质（hard / soft / diffused / dramatic）
- 最后写色温/色调（warm golden / cool blue / neon pink）
- 可包含特殊光效（god rays / volumetric light / lens flare）

示例：
```
dramatic side lighting from the left, hard shadows, warm golden tones
soft window light from the right, diffused and gentle, neutral white
rim lighting from behind, cool blue edge highlight, dark foreground
neon lighting, vibrant pink and cyan glow reflecting on wet pavement
```

---

## 字段5：构图 Composition

定义画面的景别、角度、透视关系。（本节内嵌常用表以保证本卡自足；完整术语总表见 B2-04。）

常用景别：
| 英文 | 中文 | 适用场景 |
|------|------|---------|
| extreme close-up (ECU) | 大特写 | 眼睛/嘴唇/细节 |
| close-up (CU) | 特写 | 面部/表情 |
| medium close-up (MCU) | 中近景 | 半身/肩以上 |
| medium shot (MS) | 中景 | 腰以上/对话 |
| medium wide shot (MWS) | 中远景 | 膝盖以上 |
| wide shot (WS) | 全景 | 全身+环境 |
| extreme wide shot (EWS) | 远景/大全景 | 场景全貌/交代 |

常用角度：
| 英文 | 中文 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| eye level | 平视 | 客观/中性 |
| low angle | 仰拍 | 权威/压迫/英雄感 |
| high angle | 俯拍 | 弱化/被审视 |
| bird's eye view | 鸟瞰 | 全局/抽象 |
| dutch angle / tilted | 倾斜 | 不安/紧张 |

示例：
```
medium close-up, eye level, shallow depth of field, blurred background
extreme wide shot, bird's eye view, symmetrical composition
close-up, low angle, dramatic perspective, looking up at subject
wide shot, rule of thirds, leading lines from foreground to vanishing point
```

---

## 字段6：风格 Style

定义画面的艺术风格、美学取向、时代感。

填写规则：
- 选择一个主导风格
- 可叠加1-2个修饰词增强
- 避免风格冲突（如同时写 `anime` 和 `photorealistic`）

常用风格词：
```
photorealistic, cinematic, dark fantasy, cyberpunk, retro 80s,
watercolor painting, oil painting, Studio Ghibli anime, noir,
vintage film, minimalist, surreal, gothic, steampunk, baroque
```

示例：
```
cinematic dark fantasy, moody atmospheric
retro 1980s synthwave, neon-soaked aesthetic
impressionist oil painting, thick visible brushstrokes
stark minimalist, clean geometric composition
```

---

## 字段7：画质/设备 Camera/Quality

定义模拟的拍摄设备和画质级别。

填写规则：
- 写具体相机/镜头型号更有指向性
- 写画质标签确保输出精度

常用设备：
```
shot on Arri Alexa 65, shot on RED V-Raptor, shot on Sony A7IV,
shot on Leica M11, shot on Hasselblad X2D, shot on iPhone 15 Pro
```

常用画质标签：
```
8K, ultra-detailed, hyper-realistic, sharp focus, film grain,
anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, bokeh, cinematic color grading
```

示例：
```
shot on Arri Alexa 65, anamorphic lens, 8K, cinematic color grading, film grain
shot on Sony A7IV, 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, bokeh background
8K ultra-detailed, hyper-realistic, sharp focus, studio lighting setup
```

## 七字段完整示例

### 示例1：电影人物出场
```
a tall man in his 40s wearing a long black leather coat,
standing in the rain looking straight ahead with intense eyes,
dark city street at night, puddles reflecting neon signs,
rim lighting from behind with blue edge highlight, wet reflections,
medium close-up, eye level, shallow depth of field,
neo-noir cinematic, moody atmospheric,
shot on Arri Alexa, anamorphic lens, 8K, film grain
```

### 示例2：商业产品
```
a sleek matte black wireless headphone on a marble stand,
static product display, gently floating cables suggesting movement,
pure white minimalist studio, soft gradient background,
even studio lighting, soft shadows, subtle reflections,
close-up, slight angle, macro sharp focus,
high-end commercial product photography, clean modern,
shot on Phase One IQ4, 120mm macro, 8K, ultra-detailed
```

### 示例3：奇幻场景
```
a young elven warrior with silver hair and pointed ears, wearing ornate light armor,
drawing a glowing sword from its scabbard, determined expression,
ancient forest with massive glowing mushrooms and bioluminescent vines,
soft ethereal backlight filtering through canopy, volumetric god rays,
wide shot, low angle, rule of thirds composition,
dark fantasy cinematic, epic atmospheric,
shot on RED V-Raptor, 8K, hyper-detailed, cinematic color grading
```
