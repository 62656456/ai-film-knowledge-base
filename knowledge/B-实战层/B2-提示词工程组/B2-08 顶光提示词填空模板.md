# 顶光提示词填空模板
> 用途（模板）：4种顶光场景的填空式提示词骨架模板，填入具体内容即可生成完整提示词。

## 使用方式

选定场景模板，将 `[ ]` 中的占位符替换为你的具体内容，直接拼接到七字段中对应的字段位置（七字段定义见 B2-01）。

---

## 模板一：人物肖像顶光模板

```
[Subject description], [expression/mood], 
harsh overhead lighting, deep shadows under eyes and chin, 
[background setting], [composition shot], 
[style/aesthetic], shot on [camera], [quality tags]
```

填写示例：

```
A weathered man in his 50s, intense and focused expression, 
harsh overhead lighting, deep shadows under eyes and chin, 
dim concrete interrogation room, close-up portrait, 
cinematic gritty realism, shot on Arri Alexa, 8K ultra-detailed
```

占位符说明：
- `[Subject description]`：人物外貌、年龄、服装、姿态
- `[expression/mood]`：表情和情绪状态
- `[background setting]`：背景环境描述
- `[composition shot]`：景别（close-up / medium shot / wide shot）
- `[style/aesthetic]`：风格修饰
- `[camera]`：相机型号
- `[quality tags]`：画质标签

---

## 模板二：场景氛围顶光模板

```
[Scene/Location] illuminated by [light source type], 
[light quality] top-down light, [shadow description], 
[atmosphere words], [time of day/weather], 
[additional details], [style], [quality]
```

填写示例：

```
Ancient stone temple illuminated by divine god rays, 
soft golden top-down light streaming through ceiling cracks, 
long dramatic shadows on stone floor, sacred solemn atmosphere, 
dawn dust particles floating, incense smoke catching light beams, 
dark fantasy cinematic, hyper-detailed volumetric lighting
```

占位符说明：
- `[Scene/Location]`：场景/地点
- `[light source type]`：光源类型（divine rays / fluorescent / spotlight / neon）
- `[light quality]`：光质形容词（soft / harsh / flickering / dappled）
- `[shadow description]`：阴影描述
- `[atmosphere words]`：氛围词
- `[time of day/weather]`：时间/天气补充
- `[additional details]`：额外细节（烟雾、尘埃、反光等）
- `[style]`：画面风格
- `[quality]`：画质

---

## 模板三：产品/静物顶光模板

```
[Product/Object] on [surface], 
[light type] overhead illumination, [shadow behavior], 
[background], [angle/perspective], 
[commercial style], [camera/lens], [quality]
```

填写示例：

```
Luxury perfume bottle on black marble surface, 
single focused overhead spotlight, crisp sharp shadow directly below, 
pitch black background, slightly elevated 45-degree angle, 
high-end commercial product photography, shot on Phase One IQ4, 
macro sharp focus, 8K
```

占位符说明：
- `[Product/Object]`：产品或静物描述
- `[surface]`：放置表面
- `[light type]`：顶光类型（spotlight / fluorescent / ring / natural）
- `[shadow behavior]`：阴影表现（sharp shadow / soft gradient / no shadow）
- `[background]`：背景
- `[angle/perspective]`：拍摄角度
- `[commercial style]`：商业风格
- `[camera/lens]`：设备
- `[quality]`：画质

---

## 模板四：电影角色出场顶光模板

```
[Character] [action/pose], 
[overhead lighting setup], [shadow pattern on face], 
[environment], [camera movement], 
[genre tone], [color grade], [cinema quality]
```

填写示例：

```
A lone detective standing still, 
single swinging overhead bulb, alternating light and shadow across face, 
abandoned warehouse at night, slow push-in, 
neo-noir thriller, desaturated teal and orange grade, 
Cinemascope anamorphic, film grain
```

占位符说明：
- `[Character]`：角色描述
- `[action/pose]`：动作或姿态
- `[overhead lighting setup]`：顶光设备描述
- `[shadow pattern on face]`：面部的光影图案
- `[environment]`：环境
- `[camera movement]`：镜头运动
- `[genre tone]`：类型基调
- `[color grade]`：调色风格
- `[cinema quality]`：电影质感标签
