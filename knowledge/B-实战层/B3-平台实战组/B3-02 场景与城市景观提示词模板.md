# 场景与城市景观提示词模板
> 用途（模板）：3种场景风格模板（自然风景、城市街景、室内空间），填入具体内容即可生成完整英文提示词。
> 器材说明：本卡示例中的相机/镜头/胶片型号（Sony A7R V、Hasselblad X2D、Arri Alexa、RED V-Raptor、Leica Q3、Phase One XT、Fujifilm GFX、Kodak Portra 等）是提示词的**风格标签**，用于唤起对应影调质感，非器材推荐，也不代表真实拍摄配置。

## 使用方式

选定场景类型模板，将 `[ ]` 占位符替换为具体内容。生成的提示词遵循七字段格式，可直接用于AI图片生成。

> **七字段命名口径**：本卡模板按场景需要把七字段中的 Subject 展开为「Landscape type / City type / Room type + 细节字段」，字段总数看似多于七，实为七字段的场景化展开；字段定义与命名以 B2-提示词工程组《角色与七字段定义》为准。

## 模板一：自然风景

适用场景：风景壁纸、游戏场景概念图、旅行风格化图片、电影环境交代

```
[Landscape type: mountain/ocean/forest/desert/arctic/tropical], 
[Weather and time of day: golden hour/midnight/stormy/foggy], 
[Specific terrain features: rock formations/waterfalls/meadows/cliffs], 
[Atmospheric elements: mist/clouds/stars/northern lights], 
[Lighting: sun position/moonlight/diffused/filtered through], 
[Composition: shot size, perspective, foreground-midground-background layers], 
[Style: realism/painting/fantasy/sci-fi], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, resolution, specific film or digital style]
```

填写示例1 - 山景：

```
towering snow-capped mountain peaks with a glacial lake in the valley below, 
golden hour sunset with warm orange and pink sky, 
scattered pine forests on the lower slopes, jagged rocky ridges, a small wooden cabin on the lakeshore with smoke rising from chimney, 
wispy clouds partially wrapping the peaks, light mist hovering over the lake surface, 
low-angle sunlight from the west casting long shadows, warm golden light on the peaks transitioning to cool blue in the shadows, 
extreme wide shot, slightly elevated perspective, foreground cabin leading to midground lake to background mountains, deep depth of field, 
photorealistic landscape, National Geographic style, 
shot on Sony A7R V, 16-35mm f/2.8 at 20mm, f/11, 8K, vivid natural colors
```

填写示例2 - 森林：

```
ancient redwood forest with massive tree trunks, 
early morning blue hour with soft diffused light, 
moss-covered ground, ferns and mushrooms, a narrow winding dirt path, 
low-hanging fog between the trees, dust motes floating in light shafts, 
sunlight filtering through dense canopy creating dramatic god rays, dappled light pattern on forest floor, 
wide shot, eye level looking down the path, leading lines drawing eye into depth, 
dark fantasy forest, mystical and ethereal, 
shot on Hasselblad X2D, 21mm f/4, 8K, rich deep greens and golden light beams
```

占位符填写要点：
- Landscape type 决定核心视觉，必须写具体
- Weather and time 是氛围关键，配合光线色温使用
- Composition 必须描述前景-中景-背景的层次关系

## 模板二：城市街景

适用场景：电影场景设定、城市概念图、建筑摄影、赛博朋克、复古城市

```
[City type: modern metropolis / old European town / Asian megacity / cyberpunk district / post-apocalyptic ruins], 
[Street level detail: shop fronts, signage, vehicles, pedestrians, infrastructure], 
[Time of day and weather: rain/night/rush hour/dawn], 
[Lighting: streetlights/neon/shop windows/car headlights/moonlight], 
[Composition: shot size, perspective, street depth, vanishing point], 
[Style: cinematic/photojournalistic/dystopian/vibrant], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, resolution, color grading]
```

填写示例1 - 霓虹城市夜景：

```
bustling Asian night market street lined with glowing food stalls and neon shop signs in Chinese characters, 
steam rising from noodle stalls, scattered pedestrians with umbrellas, parked scooters, hanging red lanterns, 
late night after rain, puddles reflecting colorful lights everywhere, humid atmosphere, 
vibrant neon lighting in red orange and blue, warm light spilling from shop interiors mixing with cool streetlight, 
wide shot, street-level perspective looking down the street toward vanishing point, leading lines of storefronts converging, 
cinematic night photography, Blade Runner inspired, 
shot on Arri Alexa, anamorphic lens, 8K, teal-orange color grade, subtle lens flare, film grain
```

填写示例2 - 赛博朋克街道：

```
narrow cyberpunk alley in a dystopian mega-city, 
cracked holographic billboards flickering overhead, tangled cables and pipes on building walls, a lone food vendor cart with steam, scattered debris and graffiti, 
perpetual twilight, light rain, puddles on cracked asphalt, 
neon pink and cyan light from signs, harsh underlight from vendor cart glowing green, reflections on wet surfaces, 
medium wide shot, low angle looking up the alley, vertical perspective emphasizing towering buildings, 
cyberpunk dystopian, gritty and atmospheric, 
shot on RED V-Raptor, 14mm wide-angle, 8K, high contrast, saturated neon colors, chromatic aberration
```

填写示例3 - 欧洲老城晨光：

```
cobblestone street in a medieval Italian village, 
terracotta rooftops, ivy-covered stone walls, a small fountain in a piazza, an elderly man walking a dog, flower pots on window sills, 
early morning golden hour, clear sky with wispy clouds, 
warm directional sunlight from the east, long soft shadows from buildings, gentle fill light bouncing off warm-colored walls, 
medium wide shot, eye level looking toward the piazza, symmetrical street narrowing in the distance, 
travel photography, warm nostalgic European atmosphere, 
shot on Leica Q3, 28mm f/1.7, 8K, natural warm color palette, subtle film grain
```

占位符填写要点：
- City type 必须明确时代和地域（赛博朋克 / 中世纪 / 现代 / 复古）
- Street level detail 是画面丰富度的关键，至少写3个以上具体元素
- Lighting 城市场景注意多光源混合（霓虹+路灯+车灯+橱窗光）

## 模板三：室内空间

适用场景：室内设计概念、电影室内场景、建筑摄影、家居/商业空间

```
[Room type: living room/studio/restaurant/warehouse/chapel/library], 
[Key furniture and decor: specific pieces, materials, textures], 
[Window/treatment: natural light source, curtains, view outside], 
[Atmospheric detail: plants, books, art, personal items], 
[Lighting: primary source (window/overhead/lamp), secondary fill, mood], 
[Composition: shot size, perspective from which corner, depth layers], 
[Style: interior design aesthetic, era], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, white balance, resolution]
```

填写示例1 - 现代公寓客厅：

```
spacious modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows, 
a charcoal gray sectional sofa, walnut coffee table with art books, a single large abstract painting on white wall, 
morning sunlight streaming through large windows with sheer white curtains, view of city skyline beyond glass, 
a monstera plant in the corner, a ceramic vase with dried eucalyptus on the coffee table, architectural magazines stacked neatly, 
bright natural light flooding from east-facing windows, soft shadows from window mullions, warm ambient light bouncing off light wood floors, 
wide shot, standing near the window looking back into the room, deep depth of field from foreground plant to background sofa, 
modern interior design, Scandinavian minimalist, warm and airy, 
shot on Phase One XT, 28mm f/5.6, 8K, natural white balance, clean sharp details
```

填写示例2 - 复古书房：

```
old-fashioned wood-paneled library study, 
floor-to-ceiling dark mahogany bookshelves packed with leather-bound books, a large carved oak desk with green banker's lamp, leather wingback chair, brass globe on a stand, 
single tall window with heavy velvet drapes partially drawn, rain streaking down the glass, 
a crystal whiskey decanter on the desk, stacks of old letters tied with string, a vintage typewriter, a Persian rug with faded patterns, 
warm tungsten light from the banker's lamp casting a green glow on the desk surface, cool blue-gray light seeping through the window, 
medium shot, angled from the doorway looking into the room, desk as focal point with bookshelves receding into shadow, 
classic dark academia, rich and textured, 
Kodak Portra 800 film simulation, 35mm f/1.4, warm amber tones, visible film grain, 8K
```

填写示例3 - 工业风餐厅：

```
industrial-chic restaurant interior with exposed brick walls and steel beams, 
communal wooden tables with mismatched vintage chairs, Edison bulb pendant lights at varying heights over the bar, an open kitchen with copper pots hanging, a concrete bar top with reclaimed wood panel, 
large industrial steel-frame windows with no curtains, warm interior visible, 
potted herbs on the windowsill, chalkboard menu on the wall, wine bottles on reclaimed shelving, 
warm amber light from Edison bulbs overhead with hard shadows, cool window light balancing from the side, mixed warm-cool contrast, 
wide shot, standing at the entrance looking toward the bar, perspective lines from floor tiles and ceiling beams converging, 
industrial loft aesthetic, warm and textured, 
shot on Fujifilm GFX 100S, 23mm f/4, 8K, rich tonal range, detailed texture rendering
```

占位符填写要点：
- Room type 必须写具体空间功能（不是泛泛的 `a room`）
- Key furniture 要写材质和颜色（不是只写 `a table`，写 `a dark walnut dining table`）
- Lighting 室内场景注意区分主光源和辅助光源
