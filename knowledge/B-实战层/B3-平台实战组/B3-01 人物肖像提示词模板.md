# 人物肖像提示词模板
> 用途（模板）：5种人物肖像风格模板，选定风格后填入具体内容即可生成完整英文提示词。
> 器材说明：本卡示例中的相机/镜头/胶片型号（Arri Alexa、Phase One、Leica M11、Sony A7R V、Kodak Portra 等）是提示词的**风格标签**，用于唤起对应影调质感，非器材推荐，也不代表真实拍摄配置。

## 使用方式

选定想要的肖像风格模板，将 `[ ]` 占位符替换为你的具体内容。生成的提示词可直接用于AI图片生成工具。所有提示词均遵循七字段格式（见 B2-提示词工程组《角色与七字段定义》）。

---

## 模板一：电影感肖像

适用场景：电影角色设定、海报人物、情绪化人像

```
[Subject: age, gender, ethnicity, hair, facial features, expression], 
[Action: pose, gaze direction, body language], 
[Environment: setting description, background details], 
[Lighting: light direction, quality, color temperature], 
[Composition: shot size, angle, depth of field], 
[Style: genre aesthetic, mood], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, resolution, grading]
```

填写示例：

```
a man in his 40s with short grey-streaked hair and a thin scar across his left eyebrow, intense brooding expression, 
standing with hands in pockets looking slightly to the left, 
narrow alley between brick buildings at dusk, steam rising from ground-level vents, 
dramatic Rembrandt lighting from the right, warm golden tones mixing with cool blue shadows, 
medium close-up, eye level, shallow depth of field, brick wall blurred behind, 
neo-noir cinematic, melancholic and gritty, 
shot on Arri Alexa 65, 85mm lens, 8K, desaturated teal-orange color grade, subtle film grain
```

占位符填写要点：
- Subject 必须包含年龄、发型、面部特征、表情
- Lighting 建议查阅光源速查资料选择合适光位（见 B2-提示词工程组《光源方向中英文对照表》）
- Camera/Quality 写具体相机型号效果更好

## 模板二：商业时尚肖像

适用场景：品牌代言、时尚大片、社交媒体头像、杂志封面

```
[Subject: age, gender, ethnicity, hair style, makeup, outfit brand/designer], 
[Action: pose, expression, attitude], 
[Environment: studio or location, backdrop color/texture], 
[Lighting: studio setup, modifier type, evenness], 
[Composition: shot size, angle, framing style], 
[Style: fashion aesthetic, era/vibe], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, commercial quality tags]
```

填写示例：

```
a young East Asian woman in her mid-20s with sleek bob-cut black hair, dewy glowing skin, wearing a cream white oversized blazer and gold statement earrings, 
looking directly at camera with a confident slight smile, chin slightly raised, 
clean white seamless backdrop, subtle shadow beneath feet, 
even butterfly lighting with large softbox, soft fill from both sides, neutral white with hint of warmth, 
medium close-up, straight-on angle, centered composition, razor-sharp focus, 
high-end fashion editorial, modern minimalist luxury, 
shot on Phase One IQ4 150MP, 110mm f/2.8 Schneider lens, commercial retouched skin, 8K ultra-crisp
```

占位符填写要点：
- Subject 服装和妆容要具体到品牌风格或设计师风格
- Lighting 商业人像优先用蝴蝶光或柔光正面光
- Style 不混搭时代感（如不要同时写复古80s和极简现代）

## 模板三：环境人像

适用场景：职业展示、生活方式、故事感人像、旅行摄影

```
[Subject: age, gender, appearance, clothing suited to environment], 
[Action: doing something natural to the environment, expression], 
[Environment: detailed location with specific elements and atmosphere], 
[Lighting: natural or available light description, time of day], 
[Composition: shot size, how subject relates to environment, leading lines], 
[Style: lifestyle/documentary aesthetic], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, film stock or digital style]
```

填写示例：

```
an elderly Japanese man in his 70s wearing a dark blue work apron and round spectacles, 
carefully arranging a bonsai tree on a wooden stand, focused and calm expression, 
traditional Japanese workshop interior, wooden shelves filled with ceramic pots and pruning tools, morning light streaming through paper shoji screens, 
soft natural window light from the left, warm golden morning glow, gentle shadows, 
medium shot, rule of thirds with subject on left third, workshop depth receding to the right, 
lifestyle documentary, warm nostalgic Japanese aesthetic, 
shot on Leica M11, 50mm Summilux, natural color palette, subtle film grain
```

占位符填写要点：
- Subject 服装必须与Environment匹配（厨师穿围裙、医生穿白大褂等）
- Environment 要有具体细节元素，不能只写 `a room`
- Lighting 优先用自然光（window light / golden hour / ambient）

## 模板四：暗调艺术肖像

适用场景：情绪表达、创意海报、音乐人宣传照、概念人像

```
[Subject: appearance, distinctive features, expression conveying emotion], 
[Action: pose suggesting inner state, body tension or relaxation], 
[Environment: minimal or abstract dark background], 
[Lighting: single source dramatic lighting, strong contrast], 
[Composition: shot size, negative space usage, cropping], 
[Style: art photography aesthetic, mood descriptor], 
[Camera/Quality: camera, lens, post-processing style]
```

填写示例：

```
a young woman with shaved head and intricate geometric tattoos on her scalp, lips slightly parted, eyes looking upward with a haunting expression, 
head tilted back, hands loosely at sides, 
pitch black background with faint smoke wisps, 
single overhead spotlight creating a cone of light, deep shadows engulfing the body, high contrast, 
close-up, centered, dark negative space surrounding the illuminated face, 
fine art portrait, dark and haunting, ethereal yet unsettling, 
shot on Sony A7R V, 85mm f/1.4 GM, high contrast black and white conversion, deep shadows crushed, 8K
```

占位符填写要点：
- Subject 特征要鲜明有辨识度（纹身、疤痕、独特发型等）
- Lighting 只用一个主光源，不用补光，保持高对比
- Environment 保持极简或纯黑，让人物成为唯一焦点

## 模板五：复古人像

适用场景：复古风格项目、怀旧主题、特定年代再现

```
[Subject: appearance styled to specific era, hair, makeup, outfit], 
[Action: period-appropriate pose and expression], 
[Environment: era-accurate setting or period studio backdrop], 
[Lighting: period-appropriate light quality and color], 
[Composition: shot size, era-typical framing], 
[Style: specific decade aesthetic, film stock reference], 
[Camera/Quality: vintage camera simulation or actual film stock tag]
```

填写示例：

```
a woman in her late 20s with victory roll curls and bold red lipstick, wearing a 1940s navy blue A-line dress with white collar, 
sitting with hands clasped on lap, poised elegant smile looking at camera, 
vintage studio with draped velvet curtain backdrop in deep burgundy, ornate brass frame visible at edge, 
soft warm tungsten light with gentle butterfly shadow under nose, golden amber tones, 
medium close-up, centered formal pose, slightly low angle, 
1940s Hollywood glamour, pin-up elegance, 
Kodak Portra 400 film stock, medium format simulation, natural skin tones, warm grain
```

占位符填写要点：
- 所有元素必须统一到同一个年代（服装、发型、妆容、场景、光线色温）
- Style 写具体年代（如 `1970s`、`Victorian era`、`1920s flapper`）
- Camera/Quality 用胶片型号标签（如 `Kodak Portra 400`、`Fuji Pro 400H`、`Ilford HP5`）增强复古感
